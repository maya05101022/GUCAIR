About GUCAIR

A shared, transparent approach to institutional AI readiness.

The Global University Consortium of AI Readiness is an academic platform for structured self-assessment, peer comparison and practical improvement. It is designed for universities at every stage of AI adoption.

Mission

Provide universities with a rigorous, comparable and practical way to understand institutional AI readiness and convert that understanding into responsible action.

Vision

A global academic community where AI adoption is guided by evidence, shared standards, ethical governance and open peer learning.

Values

Transparency in method, honesty about limitations, responsible use of data, institutional autonomy and collaboration across borders and disciplines.

The assessment instrument

Six dimensions, 24 indicators, one consistent scale.

Every institution answers the same 24 questions. This consistency is what makes comparison meaningful. Responses describe current institutional practice, not aspiration, and should be supported by evidence available to the responding team.

ScaleMaturity levelWhat it means
0Not ImplementedNo activity, capability or formal arrangement exists.
1InitialIsolated experiments or informal activity without institutional support.
2DevelopingDefined activity in some units with partial resources and coordination.
3EstablishedInstitution-wide practice, documented and resourced with routine review.
4Fully ImplementedSystematic, measured, continuously improved and externally recognised.

How scores are calculated

Documented method

Dimension score = (sum of dimension responses ÷ (4 × number of dimension questions)) × 100.

Overall score = (sum of all responses ÷ (4 × 24)) × 100.

Category = Initial (0–39), Developing (40–59), Advanced (60–79), Leading (80–100).

Data and limitations

Honest about what this platform is — and is not.

Indicative assessment. GUCAIR readiness scores are self-reported, generated by this platform and intended for institutional reflection. They are not official accreditation, ranking or regulatory certification.

Institutional records are visible only to authorised representatives of that institution and consortium administrators. Consortium analytics present aggregated data from approved institutions. Demonstration records, where present, are explicitly labelled and must not be interpreted as real consortium membership.

The platform does not invent partnerships, endorsements, rankings or external affiliations. Resources are curated educational material; external links are references, not endorsements.
